# Eligibility and Teaming 































### Who may enter 

The Competition is open to full-time students (including undergraduates, master's and doctoral candidates) currently enrolled at overseas universities and research institutions, with 60% non-Chinese nationals, major, or year of study. For the purposes of these Rules, "overseas universities" refers to higher education institutions and research organizations established in countries other than China (including regions outside Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR, and Chinese Taipei) that hold independent accreditation and are authorized to independently confer academic qualifications or degrees. 

### Teaming 

Teams of 2–5 students; cross-year, cross-major, cross-university teaming (including cross-border collaborations) is welcome, provided that all team members are from institutions outside Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR, and Chinese Taipei. Each participant may join only one team across both the Domestic and International Tracks; duplicate registration is invalid. 

### Language 

English is the official language of the International Track; submissions, presentations, and reviews are all conducted in English. 

Note: To ensure the international character of this track, at least 60% of a team’ s members should be non-Chinese nationals. Track assignment is determined by the organizer based on members’ nationalities and institutions.
